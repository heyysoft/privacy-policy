Sproutlines: Pipe Puzzles

Privacy Policy

This page explains what data the Sproutlines: Pipe Puzzles game ("Sproutlines") stores and accesses, how advertising in the game works, and what choices you have.

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Information We Store and Access

Sproutlines does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location permission.

The game stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Game progress: the level you are playing, the pipes you have placed on it, and which levels you have completed.
  • Hints: how many free hints you have left.
  • Settings: your Sound and Haptics preferences.
  • Garden League: the points you earned today, the levels that earned them, and yesterday's rank. The other players in the Garden League are made up by the game on your device; your score is not sent to anyone.
  • Advertising state: whether the tracking permission request has already been shown.

Heyy Software does not upload your progress, hints, settings, league points, or advertising state to its own servers. Device backups and restore features are controlled separately by Apple and by your device settings.

2. Advertising

Sproutlines is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. The game shows:

  • Rewarded ads: when you have no free hints left, tapping Hint plays a short ad, and you receive a hint when the ad has finished. Rewarded ads are only shown when you tap Hint.
  • Interstitial ads: from level 6 on, a full-screen ad may be shown at the end of a level, before the next level opens. No full-screen ad is shown at the end of levels 1 to 5.
  • Banner ads: a banner ad may be shown at the bottom of the game screen while you play. It is not shown on the home screen, on the Garden League screen, or while Settings is open.

To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device, app, or developer-related identifiers; on iOS, access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) is subject to Apple's tracking permission
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system version, and app information
  • Advertising data and interactions, such as ad views, video views, and taps
  • App interactions, including app launches
  • Crash logs and performance information, such as launch time, hangs, and energy use

The Google Mobile Ads SDK processes this information under Google's privacy terms to provide, measure, and improve its advertising services. Heyy Software can view advertising reports in AdMob but does not operate its own server to receive device advertising identifiers. Google's description of SDK data use is available in its iOS data disclosure guide.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored or accessed by the game is used only for the following purposes:

  • To save your progress so you can continue where you left off
  • To keep your hints, settings, and Garden League points for the day
  • To show ads and give you a hint for a rewarded ad
  • To respect your consent and tracking choices
  • To respond when you choose to contact support

4. Consent and Tracking Choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form when the app starts. You may change your choice at any time using the Privacy Options entry in the game's Settings, which appears when it applies to you.

On iOS, the game may ask for permission to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). This request is not shown when you first open the app; it is shown once, when you first open level 6 or a later level. Until you answer, ads are requested without personalization. If you do not allow tracking, the game requests non-personalized ads without using the IDFA for tracking. Rejecting tracking does not stop you from playing, and it does not guarantee that an ad will be shown.

You can change the tracking permission at any time in your device settings (on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Tracking permission and the choices in Google's consent form are separate controls.

5. Data Sharing

Heyy Software does not operate a player-account service or an online game-progress service for Sproutlines. The local game records described above are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Information may nevertheless be processed in the following situations:

  • When Google AdMob collects data directly in order to show ads, as described above
  • When required to comply with legal obligations
  • When processing occurs within Apple's app store, operating system, or backup infrastructure
  • When you voluntarily send support email: we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include, and use them to handle your request

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game files are stored in the app's device storage. Reset Progress in Settings deletes your game progress and your Garden League points; your hints, settings, and the record of the tracking request stay. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. This does not delete information already processed by Google or email you have sent to support.

Google controls retention of information it processes under its privacy policy. Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to those obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Sproutlines is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The game does not require a player account or ask players to submit their name, email address, or photos to play. Advertising services may process device and usage data as explained above.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us using the details below so we can review and address the request, including deleting information where appropriate.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are taken to help protect user data. However, no method of data processing over the internet, mobile devices, or platform services can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, whose data processing activities are subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Apple's operating system, App Store, and any backup services you choose to use
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts these web pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes: GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google's Gmail service, used for support email: Google Privacy Policy

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights:

  • To learn what data is being processed
  • To withdraw consent or permissions you previously granted
  • To opt out of personalized advertising
  • To delete your local game data with Reset Progress or by deleting the game
  • To contact us and request information

You may manage ad consent from the Privacy Options entry in the game's Settings, and tracking permission from your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. If a change requires a new permission or consent choice, the relevant choice will be requested separately; merely continuing to play is not treated as consent to tracking or to a new purpose requiring consent.
