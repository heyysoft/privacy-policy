Marble Pop

Privacy Policy

This page explains what data the Marble Pop game stores and accesses, how advertising in the game works, and what choices you have over these permissions and processing activities.

Status: Review Draft Last Updated: November 2, 2026
Review draft — not legal advice.

This draft describes the current iOS game and its advertising services. The developer should review it against the final release, intended audience, and website setup before adopting it as the published privacy policy.

1. Information We Store and Access

Marble Pop does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location permission.

The game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Game progress: The state of your current game, so that you can continue it later.
  • Best score: Your highest score.
  • Settings: Your Music, Sound Effects, and Haptics preferences.
  • Advertising counters: Small counters such as the number of finished games and when an ad was last shown, used to limit how often ads appear, and whether the tracking permission request has already been shown.

Heyy Software does not upload your saved game, best score, settings, or local advertising counters to its own servers. Device backups and restore features are controlled separately by Apple and by your device settings.

2. Advertising

Marble Pop is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. The game shows:

  • Optional rewarded ads: You may choose to watch an ad to receive extra in-game tools. Rewarded ads are never shown without your action.
  • Interstitial ads: A full-screen ad may occasionally be shown after a game ends, when you choose Play Again or Main Menu. The frequency of these ads is limited.

The game does not show banner ads.

To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device, app, or developer-related identifiers; on iOS, access to the IDFA is subject to Apple's tracking permission
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system version, and app information
  • Advertising data and interactions, such as ad views, video views, and taps
  • App interactions, including app launches
  • Crash logs and performance information, such as launch time, hangs, and energy use

The Google Mobile Ads SDK processes this information under Google's privacy terms to provide, measure, and improve its advertising services. Heyy Software can view advertising reports in AdMob but does not operate its own server to receive device advertising identifiers. Google's description of SDK data use is available in its iOS data disclosure guide.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored or accessed by the game is used only for the following purposes:

  • To save your game so you can continue it later
  • To keep your best score and settings
  • To limit how often ads are shown
  • To show ads and give you rewards for rewarded ads
  • To respect your consent and tracking choices
  • To respond when you choose to contact support

4. Consent and Tracking Choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. You may change your choice at any time using the Privacy Options entry in the game's Settings screen, which appears when it applies to you.

On iOS, the game may ask for permission to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). This request is shown after your first finished game, not at launch. If you do not allow tracking, the game requests non-personalized ads without using the IDFA for tracking. Ads may still be available where the applicable consent settings permit them; rejecting tracking does not guarantee that an ad will be shown.

You can change the tracking permission at any time in your device settings (on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Tracking permission and the choices in Google's consent form are separate controls.

5. Data Sharing

Heyy Software does not operate a player-account service or an online game-progress service for Marble Pop. The local game records described above are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Information may nevertheless be processed in the following situations:

  • When Google AdMob collects data directly in order to show ads, as described above
  • When required to comply with legal obligations
  • When processing occurs within Apple's app store, operating system, or backup infrastructure
  • When you voluntarily send support email: we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include, and use them to handle your request

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game files are stored in the app's device storage. Starting a New Game replaces the current game but keeps your best score and preferences; local backup copies may retain previous states. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. This does not delete information already processed by Google or email you have sent to support.

Google controls retention of information it processes under its privacy policy. Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to those obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Marble Pop is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The game does not require a player account or ask players to submit their name, email address, or photos to play. Advertising services may process device and usage data as explained above.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us using the details below so we can review and address the request, including deleting information where appropriate.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are taken to help protect user data. However, no method of data processing over the internet, mobile devices, or platform services can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, whose data processing activities are subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Apple's operating system, App Store, and any backup services you choose to use
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts these web pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes: GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google's Gmail service, used for support email: Google Privacy Policy

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights:

  • To learn what data is being processed
  • To withdraw consent or permissions you previously granted
  • To opt out of personalized advertising
  • To delete your local game data by deleting the game
  • To contact us and request information

You may manage ad consent from the Privacy Options entry in the game's Settings, and tracking permissions from your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. If a change requires a new permission or consent choice, the relevant choice will be requested separately; merely continuing to play is not treated as consent to tracking or to a new purpose requiring consent.
