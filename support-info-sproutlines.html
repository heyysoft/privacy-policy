Sproutlines Support

For questions, problems, or feedback about Sproutlines: Pipe Puzzles, contact Heyy Software:

heyysoftware@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version and the level number
  • A description of what happened and the steps that led to it

You can change your ad consent choice with Privacy Options in the game's Settings, when it appears for you, and your tracking permission in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Include only information relevant to your request. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us on behalf of a child.

Support email may contain your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to handle your request.
