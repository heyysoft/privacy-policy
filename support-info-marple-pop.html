For questions, problems, or feedback about Marble Pop, contact Heyy Software:

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version and a description of what happened
  • The steps that led to the problem

Include only information relevant to your request. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us on behalf of a child.

Support email may contain your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to handle your request.
